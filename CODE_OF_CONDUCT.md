# Code of Conduct

This code is the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, adapted for Guarana. It applies to everyone who takes part: maintainers, contributors, testers, and the people who run AI agents here, who answer for what their agents post.

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and leaders, pledge to make taking part in this community free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that keep this community open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps build a good community:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respecting opinions, viewpoints and experiences that differ from your own
- Giving constructive feedback, and taking it gracefully
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing to those they affected, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the whole community, not only for ourselves
- Crediting other people's work, here and upstream

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Sharing or asking for keys, firmware, game files or links to them, or help with piracy (see [LEGAL.md](LEGAL.md))
- Flooding issues or pull requests with unreviewed, machine-generated content
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainers are responsible for explaining and enforcing these standards, and take fair and appropriate action against behaviour they find inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and will say why when that is appropriate.

## Scope

This code applies in every space of the project (the repository, its issues, pull requests and Discussions, and any chat or site the project runs) and when someone represents the project in public, for example from an official account or as a named representative at an event.

## Reporting

Report behaviour that breaks this code privately to the maintainers:

- through GitHub's private reporting at <https://github.com/karimalsalah/guarana/security/advisories/new>, which only maintainers can read (start the title with "Conduct:"), or
- by contacting a maintainer directly, such as [@karimalsalah](https://github.com/karimalsalah).

Every report is reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainers keep the reporter's privacy and the details of the incident confidential.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainers follow these guidelines to decide the consequences of a breach of this code.

### 1. Correction

**Impact**: inappropriate language or other behaviour that is unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence**: a private written warning from a maintainer that explains the breach and why it was inappropriate. A public apology may be asked for.

### 2. Warning

**Impact**: a breach through a single incident or a series of actions.

**Consequence**: a warning with consequences for continued behaviour. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing this code, for a set time; this includes avoiding interaction in community spaces and external channels such as social media. Breaking these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Impact**: a serious breach, including sustained inappropriate behaviour.

**Consequence**: a temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community for a set time. No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing this code, is allowed meanwhile. Breaking these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Impact**: a pattern of breaches, such as sustained inappropriate behaviour, harassment of a person, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people.

**Consequence**: a permanent ban from any public interaction within the community.
