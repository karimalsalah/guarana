On a low-end PC?

Guarana targets integrated graphics, the kind in most laptops and handhelds, rather than gaming graphics cards. Switch 2 emulation is still demanding: our one measured result is 22.83 game fps in an outdoor scene on a Core Ultra 9 285H laptop with Arc 140T. Older or lower-power iGPUs are untested, so we can't promise playable speeds on them.

The fastest way to find out is to try the starting settings and send a report, good or bad.
