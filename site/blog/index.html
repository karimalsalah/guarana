    How a shader fix took Hyrule Field from 14.8 to 22.8 fps on an Intel Arc iGPU

    Translated shaders were searching the bound buffers for memory accesses they could not resolve ahead of time. Routing those accesses better cut a foliage draw from 15.7 to 5.8 ms on an Arc 140T, with the limits of one laptop and one scene.

    Why 16.67 ms is hard: frame pacing and frame generation on a 120 Hz OLED

    Frame generation was on and motion still stuttered. The diagnosis (forced FIFO, a present thread not locked to vblank, fixed-fraction prediction) and the vblank-locked presenter being built to fix it.

    Booting a Switch 2 emulator in half the time: what the boot marks found

    Timing marks on every boot phase show about 11 s of a 24.7 s boot going to Vulkan instance creation. What the marks log, what is being cut, and how to measure your own boot.
