Legal

What Guarana is, what it isn't, and how to reach us about rights.

No keys, no firmware, no games

Guarana contains no encryption keys, no system firmware and no games, and it never links to places that offer them. To play, you dump keys, firmware and games from a Nintendo Switch 2 and games you own. We don't help anyone obtain them in any other way: issues, pull requests and comments that ask for them or share them are closed and removed.

Takedown requests

The code, the releases and this website are hosted by GitHub. If you believe something here infringes your copyright, send a notice through GitHub's DMCA takedown policy (see how to submit a notice). GitHub reviews the notice and contacts us, and the same process handles counter-notices. For any other rights concern, open an issue, leaving out private details. We act on valid requests promptly.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party scripts or fonts. It is served by GitHub Pages, and GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Not legal advice

Laws on emulation and on copying your own games differ by country. This page describes the project, not your rights. If in doubt, ask a lawyer where you live.
