# Legal

## Not affiliated

Guarana is an independent, open-source project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Nintendo, Intel, AMD, NVIDIA or Microsoft, nor by the upstream projects it builds on (Lichii, Eglantine, Eden, yuzu), which keep their own names and responsibilities.

## Nothing proprietary is included or allowed

Guarana provides no keys (`prod.keys`, `title.keys` or others), no firmware, no system files or system archives, no BIOS, and no games. It cannot play anything without them.

You must dump your own keys, firmware and games from hardware and software you own, with tools and methods that are legal where you live. Nobody in this project will give them to you, sell them, or tell you where to find them.

They are not allowed anywhere in this project: not in the repository, in issues, pull requests, Discussions, release files or CI artifacts, nor as links, hashes that identify a copy, or torrent or file-host references. Neither are extracted game data (RomFS, ExeFS, textures, models, audio, video) or dumps of game code, shaders or memory beyond the minimum a bug needs, which stays on the reporter's machine. Maintainers delete such content and may ban whoever posts it again.

## No piracy

Discussion of piracy, of getting games, keys or firmware you do not own, of getting around copy protection, or of "where do I find" requests is not allowed, and is closed or deleted. The [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) treats it as unacceptable behaviour.
